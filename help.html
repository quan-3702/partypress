🎈 How to use PartyPress

PartyPress makes a whole set of matching birthday party printables with your child's name and age. Everything stays on this computer.

1. Party details

Type the name, the age, the date, time, place and how to RSVP. They are filled in on every printable.

2. Pick a theme

Confetti and Balloons are free. Pro adds Dinosaurs, Outer space, Under the sea, Daisies, Rainbow and Groovy disco.

3. Choose what to print

  • Invitations – two 5 × 7 in cards per sheet. Edit the words and add a photo if you like.
  • Birthday banner – one big letter on each flag. Change the banner words to anything, e.g. Happy 5th Birthday Mia.
  • Cupcake toppers – 20 per sheet with the age, the name and "Happy Birthday". Tape a toothpick to the back.
  • Favor tags – nine per sheet, with a hole to punch.
  • Welcome sign, food labels, thank-you cards and bottle labels (Pro).

4. Print

Click 🖨 Print. In the print window set Scale to 100% / "Actual size" and turn off "Fit to page". Card stock (65–110 lb / 176–300 gsm) looks best for invitations, toppers and tags. Choose "Save as PDF" to keep a file.

Want a big welcome sign? With Pro, click 💾 Save as picture and print it at a photo shop.
