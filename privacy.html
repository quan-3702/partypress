PartyPress – Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

PartyPress does not collect, send or sell any personal information. Party details and photos you add are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They never leave your device unless you print or save a file yourself.

PartyPress has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Purchases of PartyPress Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store. PartyPress only asks the Store whether Pro is owned; it never sees your payment details.

You can delete your data at any time with ☰ More → Start a new party, or by uninstalling the app.
